## What is not justified as a scientific conclusion

- Agreement of a predicted reciprocal peak with one bright region is not proof of composition, strain, phase or epitaxy.
- An omega pseudo-Voigt FWHM is not automatically a mosaicity or crystallite-size result. No resolution deconvolution or broadening model is implemented.
- Per-map normalized logarithmic color is not an absolute-intensity comparison between specimens.
- Absence of a plotted peak or temperature point is not evidence that it was not measured: branches and plot filters may suppress it.
- Correct Bragg-law numbers do not validate scan zeroes, handedness or use of direct versus reciprocal orientation vectors.
- Missing explicit intensity corrections locally does not prove the source export lacked them.
